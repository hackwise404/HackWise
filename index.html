FAQ
FAQ

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do I need a technical background to join?+

No. Our CJCP program starts from absolute fundamentals — networking, Linux, and Windows basics — before moving into hacking concepts. Anyone with curiosity and willingness to practice can join.

Are the classes online, offline, or hybrid?+

We offer both offline classroom training at our Thiruvananthapuram campus and live online sessions, so you can choose whichever fits your schedule and location.

Will I get hands-on lab access?+

Yes. Every program includes dedicated lab time on real tools like Kali Linux, Metasploit, Wireshark, and more, along with guided practical projects so you build a portfolio, not just theory.

Do you provide placement assistance?+

Yes. Our career development track covers resume building, LinkedIn optimization, mock interviews, and technical interview prep, backed by direct hiring-partner connections and referral support.

What certification will I receive after completion?+

You'll receive a HackWise Academy certification specific to your program (CJCP, CCSA, or COSP), along with project completion certificates that showcase your practical work to employers.

Can I switch or upgrade my program later?+

Yes, many students start with a foundational program like CJCP and later upgrade into CCSA or COSP as their skills and interests develop. Speak with our team about pathway options.

Is there a payment plan or EMI option available?+

Yes, flexible installment options are available for most programs. Reach out through the enquiry form or WhatsApp and our team will share the plan that suits your program.
